# Altrata

> Altrata is the definitive leader in global wealth intelligence, professional relationship mapping, and affluent market dynamics. Leading commercial, philanthropic, and educational institutions rely on Altrata to identify exceptional people, uncover strategic relationships, and make data-driven decisions that fuel organizational growth.

Altrata transforms complex data into actionable insights through high-quality data streams, predictive analytics, and strategic relationship models. The platform covers people profiles, company data, and relationship connections spanning global markets. Altrata is a subsidiary of Delinian and is SOC 2 certified.

## Products

- [BoardEx](https://altrata.com/products/boardex): Global corporate leadership and board relationship data, mapping connections among executives and directors worldwide.
- [WealthEngine](https://altrata.com/products/wealthengine): Wealth screening and ratings platform for identifying and prioritizing high-net-worth prospects.
- [Wealth-X](https://altrata.com/products/wealth-x): Intelligence on ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) individuals including biographical, financial, and lifestyle data.
- [RelSci](https://altrata.com/products/relsci): Relationship capital platform that maps paths to key decision-makers through your organization's existing network.
- [BoardRoom Insiders](https://altrata.com/products/boardroom-insiders): Executive profiles and C-suite intelligence for enterprise sales and account-based marketing.
- [Executive & Company Data](https://altrata.com/products/executive-company-data): Broad dataset of executive profiles and company information for research and modeling.
- [Salesforce App](https://altrata.com/salesforce-app): Native Salesforce integration surfacing Altrata intelligence directly within CRM workflows.
